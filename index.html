夏玉生Yusheng Xia

上海交通大学 · 博士研究生 Ph.D. Candidate, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

夏玉生，上海交通大学博士研究生，本科毕业于河南农业大学，硕士毕业于中国农业科学院作物科学研究所。主要从事基因组学与作物复杂性状解析研究，研究方向涵盖泛基因组构建与分析、单细胞转录组学及空间转录组学，致力于整合多组学大数据，揭示作物重要农艺性状的遗传基础与调控机制，为分子设计育种提供理论支撑。 Yusheng Xia is a Ph.D. candidate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. He received his B.S. degree from Henan Agricultural University and his M.S. degree from the Institute of Crop Sciences, Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences. His research focuses on genomics and the dissection of complex traits in crops, spanning pan-genome construction and analysis, single-cell transcriptomics, and spatial transcriptomics. By integrating multi-omics data, he aims to unravel the genetic basis and regulatory mechanisms underlying key agronomic traits, providing theoretical support for molecular design breeding.

研究方向Research Directions

泛基因组学Pan-genomics

构建作物泛基因组与图基因组，解析结构变异与基因存在/缺失变异，挖掘栽培种与野生近缘种的遗传多样性。 Constructing crop pan-genomes and graph genomes to resolve structural and presence/absence variation, mining genetic diversity across cultivars and wild relatives.

单细胞转录组Single-cell Transcriptomics

绘制作物细胞图谱，鉴定稀有细胞类型，推断发育轨迹与细胞命运决定的调控网络。 Charting crop cell atlases, identifying rare cell types, and inferring developmental trajectories and the regulatory networks that determine cell fate.

空间转录组Spatial Transcriptomics

在空间维度定位基因表达，解析组织微环境与细胞互作，将分子机制与农艺表型连接起来。 Mapping gene expression in its spatial context to dissect tissue microenvironments and cell–cell interactions, linking molecular mechanisms to agronomic phenotypes.
